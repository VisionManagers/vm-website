# Workbench prompt research (Aug 2026)

Distilled findings that ground the prompts in `prompts.ts`. Benchmarks are embedded
directly in the prompts (retrieval beats recall — models hallucinate stats).
Full sourced briefs were produced by web-research agents; key data preserved here.

## Revenue Leak Audit (deals)
- Frameworks: Jay Abraham's three ways to grow (clients × transaction value × frequency; three +26% lifts compound to ~100%), agency profit-leak audit (map funnel → instrument → benchmark → dollarize delta → rank by $), Hormozi value equation to diagnose *why* a stage leaks, front-desk mystery-shopper audits (practices book only 40-60% of new-patient calls).
- Benchmarks: 62% of SMB calls unanswered; <5-min lead response = up to 391% higher conversion (21x drop 5→30 min); dental no-show 15-20% avg (top 10% ~1%); medical no-show 5-8% avg, up to 30%+ by specialty; dental case acceptance 45% avg vs 75-80% ADA target; law consult→client 14% avg, 30%+ healthy, 40-50% top; RE internet leads close 2-3% vs referrals 14-20%; HVAC/plumbing lead→job 25-45%, CSR booking 42-65% avg vs 85%+ top; dormant reactivation 3-8% single-touch vs 15-25% multi-touch; reviews: 50+ reviews = 266% more likely in Local Pack, sweet spot 4.2-4.5★ (5.0 converts 12% lower); missed legal call worth $3,500-5,200.
- Elite audits: BLUF (total $ first line), MECE stages, formula BEFORE dollar figure, ranked by $ impact (ease as separate axis), assumptions labeled (user-input vs benchmark), top-5 cap.

## Business Coach (coach)
- Frameworks: EOS IDS (identify real issue → discuss → solve w/ owner+deadline), TOC five focusing steps (identify → exploit → subordinate → elevate → repeat; steps 1-3 are free, ~60% lift before spending), Hormozi constraint ranking (rank by revenue-impact-if-solved), Martell replacement ladder (EA → delivery → marketing → sales → GM) + DRIP matrix, Profit First bands (profit 5-10%, owner comp 30-50% small-biz, opex 50-60%; ratchet 1-2%/quarter), MECE issue trees (flat revenue → new clients / avg ticket / retention / capacity).
- Session shape: Diagnose → Reframe (the question behind the question; AWE "what else") → Direct verdict → Plan → ONE accountability metric. Bungay Stanier: "If yes to this, what are you saying no to?"
- Benchmarks: marketing spend — SMB avg 7.7-9.4% of revenue; dental 5-7% stable / 7-10% growth; legal 3-7% (up to 18-21% growth mode); home services 8-12%. Owner pay: most land 20-30% of revenue ($1-3M rev → 8-15%). Automate if repeatable/rules-based (ROI 2-4 months); hire for judgment/trust (6-12 months to productivity); automate admin before hiring customer-facing. Raise prices when: booked 4-6+ wks out, turning away work, >12 months since last raise. Associate-ready (dental proxy): booked 4-8 wks, 2,000+ active patients, ≥$140k/mo production, overhead <60-65%.
- Prompt patterns: verdict not menu; explicit trade-off + "what I'd ignore"; exploit-before-invest (fix free stuff before recommending spend).

## Marketing Strategist (marketing)
- Frameworks: Dunford positioning (true competitive alternatives incl. "do nothing"/DIY; attributes with PROOF; who cares most), StoryBrand SB7 (customer = hero, business = guide, 3-step plan, direct + transitional CTA), Reeves USP (ONE claim repeated near-verbatim across every asset), JTBD trigger ("what were you doing right before you searched"), Hormozi value equation for offers (dream outcome + likelihood proof ÷ time + effort; risk-reversal element).
- Copy craft: Schwartz awareness stages — Google/LSA searchers = Solution/Product Aware (specific, offer-led); cold social/email = Unaware/Problem Aware (trigger/pain-led); review replies = Most Aware (identity reinforcement). Ogilvy: headline carries 80% of the money; promise + locality in headline. Halbert: write to ONE person, sensory concrete specifics. 7th-8th grade reading level.
- Benchmarks: Google Ads — dental CTR ~5.4%/CPC ~$8/CPL ~$73; legal CTR 5.9%/CPC ~$9.9/CPL ~$132; home services CTR 6.4%/CPC ~$8/CPL ~$91; LSA CPL: HVAC $51-80, plumbing $69, electrical $39. Email: open 35-45% inflated by MPP — use click-to-open (~6.8%) as honest metric. Reviews: 89% more likely to choose a business that replies to ALL reviews; reply within 24-48h, <75 words.
- Anti-slop: banned-word list, one hyper-local/numeric specific per asset, one CTA per asset matched to channel intent, big idea = "what your best clients already say about you."

## Lead Machine (leads)
- Frameworks: Dream 100 (for local = ~named referral partners + high-value targets; touch every 2 weeks; lumpy/memorable first touch; ask for a meeting not a sale), Hormozi core four (pick ONE channel, go deep; warm outreach: 100/day, ACA — Acknowledge, Compliment, Ask), Duct Tape referral engine (referral ask built into service delivery, not a campaign), BNI partnership mechanics (one-per-industry reciprocity, communicate your ideal-client spec so partners can pattern-match), database reactivation 3-touch (Day 1 personal re-engage / Day 3 reason+offer / Day 7 soft close; waves every 30-90 days).
- Benchmarks: local lead mix — organic 31% / maps 28% / paid search 27% / social 4%; referral close 25-40% vs cold 1-3%; GBP → customer 3.2% (vs 0.7% social); 76% of local mobile searches visit within 24h; LSA book rates ~44% (HVAC/plumbing); LTVs — dental patient $10k+ ($22-45k top practices), PI legal fee ≈ 33% × settlement (avg ~$53k), HVAC $15,340 avg ($47k with maintenance plan!), financial advisory $40-90k, RE client $75-150k over repeat+referral.
- Outreach: curiosity-framed openers beat assertions; own the interruption + ask permission; ask for referrals at the moment of praise ("helping a friend" frame: "if you ever hear someone mention…"); scoreboard = leading indicators (asks made, partner 1-on-1s, reactivations sent, review velocity), not lagging revenue.

## Deal Scout (scout)
- Interview order pros use: exit strategy FIRST → geography to submarket/zip → asset type → price + financing (cash/hard money/DSCR) → condition tolerance → thresholds (auto-suggest defaults; don't quiz beginners).
- Thresholds: 70% rule MAO = ARV × 0.7 − repairs; 1% rent rule (rare in 2025-26; 2% only cheap markets); 50% rule for instant NOI; DSCR ≥1.25 for best lender terms (1.0 floor); cap rates 2025-26: multifamily A 4.5-5.5% / B 5.5-7% / C 7-9%; industrial ~5-7%; office B 8.5-11%.
- Source productivity: probate — ~60% carry NO mortgage (vs <5% of pre-foreclosure), less competed, 15-25% better margins; NOD/pre-foreclosure most competed; list STACKING converts 3-5x single lists (tax-delinquent 2yr+ + absentee + high equity; absentee + code violation); county data best <30 days old.
- Free sources: county recorder/assessor (publicrecords.netronline.com directory), county sheriff-sale pages, Auction.com/Hubzu (~5% buyer premium), HUD Home Store (owner-occupant exclusivity window 5-15 days), Zillow pre-foreclosure filter (in default ≠ for sale).
- Quick screens: rehab $/sqft — light 15-25, moderate 25-45, heavy/gut 45-75+ (Sun Belt runs 20-40% above Midwest); deal memo fields: signals+confidence, ARV+comp basis, repair bracket, MAO vs ask, rent + DSCR/cap check, "Unknowns / Could Not Verify."
- Honesty: no MLS or paid-list access — say so; timestamp findings; Zillow "pre-foreclosure" caveat.
